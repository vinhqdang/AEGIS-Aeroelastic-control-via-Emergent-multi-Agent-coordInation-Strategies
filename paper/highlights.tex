\bigskip
\noindent What limits reinforcement learning for distributed flutter suppression,
and the credit structure that helps

\bigskip
\begin{itemize}
\item Physics-exact, closed-form credit signal for multi-agent flutter control.
\item Per-agent control power, energy-normalised, verified additive to $10^{-10}$.
\item Credit signal plus log-energy shaping beats either alone ($d=-2.70$, $p=0.002$).
\item Exploration scale, not architecture or communication, gates visible gains.
\item Matching exploration to actuation amplitude changes performance two-fold.
\end{itemize}
