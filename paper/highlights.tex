\begin{itemize}
\item Per-agent credit for multi-agent flutter suppression is derived in closed form.
\item Closed-form credit beats naive and value-decomposition baselines after correction.
\item Exploration scale, not algorithm choice, dominated performance by a factor of two.
\item Six-rung classical baseline ladder and UVLM cross-validation ground the comparison.
\item Learned policies trail decentralised LQG on average, but hold a condition it cannot.
